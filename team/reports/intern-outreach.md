## 3. What a good answer looks like (one line per question)

1. Sandbox terms: Written cost, duration and inclusions, provisioning within about one business day, and no mandatory annual commitment.
2. Rate card: Per-panel prices in INR, a validity period, and a clear statement of whether rates are fixed or can change.
3. Minimum recharge and fees: A stated minimum, refund policy, and a full list of setup and transaction fees (a written "none" is also acceptable).
4. Reseller margin: A stated margin or pricing model for resale through a platform, with conditions; "to be discussed" alone is a weak answer.
5. Mumbai coverage: A yes or no for 400067 and each named nearby pincode, with collection-slot availability; a total pincode count does not answer this.
6. API and report schema: Shared docs, the booking endpoint spec, and a sample structured report with field names, units, reference ranges and a versioning policy.
7. Webhooks and branding: A written event list (booking created, sample collected, report ready), retry behaviour, and which patient-facing elements can be branded.
8. Data and references: A DPDP-compliant data processing agreement or draft, a named data processor role, ABDM status stated as live or not live (not "ready"), and at least two customer references who will take a call.
