## Objective
Create a short, punchy, high-conviction 20-second launch video for Careloop that showcases its adherence tracking dashboard and friction-free mobile web checklist.

## Output

- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080 (30 fps)

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: High-conviction vertical SaaS launch for India's lifestyle health infrastructure
- Interpretation: Fast-in typography with comfortable settled holds; crisp clinical UI cards; confident, punchy copy without generic startup clichés.
- Angle: Dietitians spend hours writing clinical diet charts, only for PDFs to disappear into WhatsApp chaos. Careloop provides the adherence operating system for lifestyle medicine.
- Hook: "Your program doesn't end when you send the plan."
- Outro: "Careloop • The Adherence Operating System for Lifestyle Medicine."
- Avoid: Generic SaaS tropes ("streamline workflows"), unreadable flash frames, low contrast.
