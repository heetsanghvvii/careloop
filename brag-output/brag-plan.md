# Brag Plan: Careloop

## What is this app?
Careloop is a zero-app-download chronic care adherence SaaS that helps Indian dietitians and lifestyle doctors turn diet charts into daily patient follow-through via web checklists and WhatsApp deep links.

## The angle
Dietitians spend hours writing clinical diet charts, only for PDFs to get buried under family memes and forwards in the "WhatsApp Black Hole." Careloop fixes the between-consultation adherence blindspot with zero client friction.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Your program doesn't end when you send the plan." A dramatic split: a messy WhatsApp chat with unread PDFs on the left vs a clean, glowing Careloop adherence dashboard on the right.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Adherence Blindspot Solved**: Real-time triage showing active patients, 82% adherence score, and early alerts for clients drifting at Day 12.
- **Zero App-Store Friction**: 390px mobile view showing the 5-second daily checklist, streak counter (14d streak 🔥), and guilt-free rest day logging.
- **Doctor Self-Serve Onboarding**: 1 guided form that generates a fully branded clinic workspace in 2 minutes.

## Outro / punchline
"Stop patient drop-off before Day 14. Turn advice into daily adherence." Logo lockup with "Careloop • The Adherence Operating System for Lifestyle Medicine."

## User flow worth showing
Entry: Doctor prescribes PCOS & Metabolic Reset protocol → Key Action: Patient logs daily check-in with 1 tap → Result: Doctor catches at-risk patients proactively.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: High-conviction vertical SaaS launch for India's lifestyle health infrastructure
- Interpretation: Fast-in typography with comfortable settled holds; crisp clinical UI cards; confident, punchy copy without generic startup clichés.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds (600 frames at 30fps)
